# Manuscript Packaging Move

## Use when

Published research state is stable enough that a specific manuscript architecture, section, figure/table placement, reference integration, or final-deliverable packaging gap is the Critical Path bottleneck.

## Required work

1. Name the exact manuscript obligation and source line/campaign evidence.
2. Use only published accepted/qualified cards and visible limitations.
3. Update the staged blueprint or paper plan at the correct local position.
4. Keep planned evidence in PAPER_PLAN; do not fabricate result text in BLUEPRINT.
5. Place active figures/tables/methods/results with provenance and reader takeaway.
6. Follow target venue profile and reference requirements.
7. Preserve distinction between architecture, draft prose, and final readiness.

## Local completeness

Complete when the named manuscript gap is closed or diagnosed. It does not require writing the full paper unless the charter and final campaign specifically call for that bounded deliverable.

## Result cards

Typical cards: artifact, design decision, claim revision, boundary condition.
