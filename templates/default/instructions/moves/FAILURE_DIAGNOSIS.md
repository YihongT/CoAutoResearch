# Failure Diagnosis Move

## Use when

A prior method, experiment, acquisition, pipeline, or claim failed and the next decision depends on understanding why.

## Required work

1. Distinguish execution failure, measurement failure, data failure, method failure, and hypothesis failure.
2. List plausible competing causes.
3. Design the smallest discriminating checks.
4. Preserve logs and examples that support or rule out causes.
5. Assign confidence to the diagnosis.
6. Decide whether to repair, narrow scope, change method, change metric, abandon a line, or ask a human.
7. Record explanations not ruled out.

## Local completeness

Complete when the diagnosis changes the rational next action, even if the original result remains unsuccessful.

## Result cards

Typical cards: diagnostic insight, negative evidence, boundary condition, method decision, process lesson.

## Do not

- rerun the same failed procedure without a new discriminating hypothesis;
- claim root cause from one anecdote;
- hide a failure because it weakens the active story.
