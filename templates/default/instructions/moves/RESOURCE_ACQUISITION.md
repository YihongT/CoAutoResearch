# Resource Acquisition Move

## Use when

A research-critical dataset, paper, repository, checkpoint, benchmark definition, credential, tool, or target-venue source prevents a meaningful next move.

## Required work

1. Name the Critical Path dependency and why it is research-critical.
2. Inventory existing inspected resources before searching.
3. Follow the Resource Scout acquisition/substitution ladder.
4. Check provenance, license, access, integrity, size, freshness, and intended use.
5. Run a minimal usability/activation check when practical.
6. Record acquired, substituted, unavailable, or human-gated outcomes.
7. Keep new material raw until a report/merge promotes its research meaning.

## Local completeness

Complete when the resource is acquired and validated, a credible substitute is activated, or the ladder reaches a documented blocker with a recovery condition or human request.

## Result cards

Typical cards: resource decision, artifact, boundary condition, diagnostic insight.

## Do not

- treat a filename or link as inspected content;
- download large/restricted resources blindly;
- claim evidence from an unvalidated resource;
- use an unavailable private resource as though it exists.
