# Review Repair Move

## Use when

A concrete reviewer blocker cannot be repaired inside the originating trial without exceeding its charter, or a later trial must address a cross-cutting quality issue.

## Required work

1. Cite the exact reviewer output and blocker.
2. State the smallest repair question.
3. Distinguish evidence repair, process repair, manuscript repair, source repair, venue repair, and protocol repair.
4. Preserve the original review and provenance.
5. Execute the repair and show which blocker is resolved.
6. Rerun all reviewers whose conclusions may change.
7. Do not mark unrelated campaign gaps complete.

## Local completeness

Complete when the named blocker is resolved, superseded by a justified narrower scope, or escalated under blocked/needs-human semantics.

## Result cards

Typical cards: artifact, method decision, claim revision, process lesson.
