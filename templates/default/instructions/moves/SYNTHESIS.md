# Synthesis Move

## Use when

The next bottleneck is combining published results, resolving conflicts, choosing among research lines, revising claims, or deriving a coherent decision from multiple trials.

## Required work

1. Define the synthesis question.
2. Select accepted/qualified result cards and relevant trials.
3. List materially relevant excluded or conflicting evidence with reasons.
4. Check scope and method compatibility before combining results.
5. Separate robust conclusions from tentative interpretations.
6. Update line/claim/campaign proposals without cherry-picking.
7. State which evidence would reverse the synthesis.

## Local completeness

Complete when the specified cross-trial decision is resolved or the remaining incompatibility/human choice is explicit. It need not finalize every line or the whole manuscript.

## Result cards

Typical cards: claim revision, design decision, boundary condition, negative evidence.
