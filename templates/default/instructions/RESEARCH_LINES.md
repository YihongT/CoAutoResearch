# Research Lines

## Purpose

A research line is a defensible candidate contribution or paper story composed from published result cards. Lines allow non-linear research without losing provenance or negative evidence.

## States

- `candidate`
- `active`
- `paused`
- `candidate_final`
- `killed`
- `superseded`

Normally there is at most one active line. Killed and superseded lines are terminal; create a new line if later evidence revives an idea.

## Required content

Each line records:

- thesis;
- contribution identity;
- claim hierarchy and strength;
- supporting cards;
- limiting cards;
- conflicting cards;
- materially relevant exclusions and reasons;
- scope boundary;
- venue fit;
- current bottleneck;
- kill criteria.

## Update rules

Only published merge decisions update lines. A line-changing trial forces Full review. A line becomes `candidate_final` only when its claims, limitations, campaigns, venue posture, and deliverable architecture are sufficiently complete for a Final review candidate.

Do not hide a failed trial because it weakens the story. Use it as limiting evidence, a boundary, or a reason to kill or narrow the line.
