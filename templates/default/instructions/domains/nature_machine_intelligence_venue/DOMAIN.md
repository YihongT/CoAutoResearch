# Nature Machine Intelligence Venue Pack

## Purpose

Apply venue-specific evidence and packaging discipline when the active target is Nature Machine Intelligence. The pack is a routing and verification standard, not a cached statement of current journal policy.

## Source authority

- Inspect current official journal and publisher guidance for the exact article type.
- Record URL or stable identifier, publisher, access time, scope, and what was actually verified.
- Distinguish formal requirements from observed conventions in inspected representative articles.
- Treat requirements not supported by current official evidence as draft or unknown.
- Recheck time-sensitive limits and policies before any human submission decision.

## Fit analysis

Evaluate separately:

- audience and journal-scope fit;
- declared article-type fit;
- conceptual or empirical contribution posture;
- evidence depth relative to the claim posture;
- manuscript narrative, figures, tables, references, limitations, ethics, data/code, and supplement posture;
- predictable editorial rejection risks and how the candidate addresses them.

For a Perspective or other primarily conceptual format, do not use article type to excuse unsupported factual claims, missing literature grounding, unclear novelty, or absent boundary conditions.

## Final-gate boundary

The venue profile must be linked to the active target, schema-valid, marked ready, supported by current official evidence, and consistent with inspected representative work. Any draft, unverified, stale, mismatched, or missing profile evidence blocks a submission-ready statement.

An automated Final Gate may report only that a structured candidate passed the configured checks. It does not establish factual correctness, novelty, editorial acceptance, formal compliance, or peer-review quality. Those claims require independent human confirmation.
