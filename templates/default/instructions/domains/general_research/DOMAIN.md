# General Research Domain Pack

## Purpose

Provide the default evidence and reasoning discipline for any project while avoiding unsupported domain-specific assumptions.

## General standards

- State the research question and scope precisely.
- Distinguish observation, interpretation, hypothesis, decision, and claim.
- Match claim strength to evidence strength.
- Preserve positive, negative, null, contradictory, and limiting evidence.
- Compare alternatives when the local question depends on a design or method choice.
- Record provenance, procedures, artifacts, uncertainty, and limitations.
- Treat a diagnosed failure as useful only when the diagnosis changes a future decision.
- Do not generalize beyond the observed population, data, setting, implementation, or assumptions.
- Do not present a proxy metric as real-world utility without an explicit validity argument.
- Do not infer causality from uncontrolled association.
- Do not claim novelty without inspecting relevant prior work when novelty is central.
- Do not claim submission readiness from plans, scaffolds, or unexecuted experiment slots.

## Minimum evidence by claim posture

- Exploratory: traceable observation plus explicit uncertainty.
- Tentative: at least one credible result or analysis with limitations.
- Supported: converging evidence, appropriate comparison or validation, and addressed alternatives.
- Final for the declared deliverable: evidence package complete under project and venue standards, reviewed, and reproducible enough for human defense.

## Missing specialization

If the project needs domain-specific statistical, ethical, safety, legal, clinical, benchmark, human-subject, or other standards and no pack exists, record that gap in `EXPERT_ROUTE`. Narrow the claim or request appropriate human/specialist review; do not pretend the general pack supplies unavailable expertise.
