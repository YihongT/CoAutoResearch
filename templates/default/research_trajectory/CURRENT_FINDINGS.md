# Current Findings

This file is the latest global synthesis of research findings, results, claims,
limitations, and evidence status.

It answers: what does the research system currently believe or know?

Raw outputs, logs, figures, tables, downloaded resources, and intermediate
artifacts remain in the corresponding trial folders or workspace paths cited by
trial reports.

This file must always make clear what is latest, active, tentative,
superseded, or rejected.

---

## Status Definitions

- `active`: currently accepted or central.
- `tentative`: promising but not fully validated.
- `superseded`: replaced by newer evidence.
- `rejected`: known invalid or misleading.
- `archived`: retained for history but no longer central.
