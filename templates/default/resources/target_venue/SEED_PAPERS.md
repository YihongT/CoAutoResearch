# Target Venue Seed Papers

Seed papers are relevant papers from the target venue or target style.

They are used to learn:

- writing structure;
- motivation style;
- evidence standard;
- figure/table style;
- abstract/introduction rhythm;
- appendix/supplement organization.

Do not copy their research content.
